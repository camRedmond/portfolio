About

I'm a software engineer at Rokt, a global ecommerce adtech company. I'm currently deployed to Pay+, Rokt's payment page product, which shows shoppers relevant offers right where they enter their payment details. The role sits across ops, engineering, and product: I work directly with enterprise partners to scope and build integrations, then feed what I learn back into the product. I'm also on the launch engineering team, where I've led the development of several AI tools that help our operations teams triage and resolve issues faster.

Before Rokt, I spent close to four years at Kepler Communications, which is building an optical data relay network for satellites in orbit. I started in IT and moved into infrastructure and DevOps, where I really honed my skills in Kubernetes, CI/CD, networking, and monitoring. Kepler and its startup environment shaped how I build software: I think about how a feature gets deployed and supported, not just how it gets written.

Most of what I'm excited about right now is agents: tool calling, MCP, guardrails, evals, and figuring out where a deterministic check beats a model. I like owning a problem end to end and building things people value.

Outside of work, I enjoy golfing, staying active, reading, and building small projects that push me into new areas and teach me new skills.
